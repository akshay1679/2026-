**Azure Goat** (often written _AzureGoat_) is **not an official Microsoft product**. It’s a **community-made mascot/meme** tied to **Microsoft Azure**.

Here’s the vibe 🐐☁️

- The goat is a **playful mascot** used by Azure engineers, speakers, and fans
    
- It pops up on **conference slides, stickers, swag, and demo repos**
    
- It’s kind of an **inside joke** in the Azure community—techy but goofy
    
- Think _“Azure’s unofficial spirit animal”_
    

Why a goat?

- Goats = tough, adaptable, climb anything → perfect metaphor for cloud infra
    
- Also “GOAT” = _Greatest Of All Time_ (because engineers love wordplay)
    

Important distinction:

- ❌ **Not** an official Azure logo
    
- ❌ **Not** a product, service, or feature
    
- ✅ **Yes** to memes, swag, and community culture
