If you mean **behavior-based detection in sorting**, you’re usually talking about systems that _don’t just look at what something is_, but instead classify it based on **how it behaves over time**.

This idea shows up in several domains (security, manufacturing, logistics, email filtering, etc.), but the pattern is similar everywhere:

> observe → extract behavior → compare to models → decide where it belongs.
