### 🔹 **Definition**

- Digital forensics = process of **<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">collecting, preserving, analyzing, and reporting digital evidence</mark>**
    
- Used in **computers, mobiles, networks, cloud systems**
    
- Ensures **evidence integrity** for legal use
    

---

### 🔹 **Importance**

- Most crimes leave **digital footprints (~90%)**
    
- Helps:
    
    - Identify **what happened**
        
    - Find **who was involved**
        
    - Support **legal decisions**
        
- Protects data & ensures **cybersecurity**

---

### 🔹 **Who Uses It**

- **Law enforcement** → crime investigation
    
- **Companies** → data breaches, insider threats
    
- **Government** → national security
    
- **Cybersecurity firms** → incident response
    
- **Legal professionals** → court evidence
    

---

### 🔹 **Steps in Digital Forensics**

1. **<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Identification</mark>** → find relevant data
    
2. **<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Preservation</mark>** → keep data unchanged
    
3. **<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Collection</mark>** → gather evidence carefully
    
4. **<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Analysis</mark>** → understand events (who, what, when, how)
    
5. **<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Documentation</mark>** → record findings
    
6. **<mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Presentation</mark>** → explain evidence in court
    

---

### 🔹 **Digital Forensics vs DFIR**

- **Digital Forensics** → investigates & analyzes evidence
    
- **DFIR (Digital Forensics + Incident Response)** →
    
    - Investigates
        
    - **Responds & fixes attacks**
        

---

### 🔹 **Key Features of Good Tools**

- Evidence collection from multiple sources
    
- Artifact/data recovery
    
- Timeline analysis
    
- Memory (volatile data) analysis
    
- **Forensic soundness (legal reliability)**
    
- Easy workflow
    
- Clear reporting
    
- Integration with systems
    
- Scalability & performance
    

---

### 🔹 **Uses in Real Life**

- Cybercrime investigation
    
- Fraud detection
    
- Data breach analysis
    
- Insider threat detection
    
- Legal case support
