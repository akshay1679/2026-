## **Write Blocker (Data Blocker) – Short Definition**

A **write blocker** is a device or software used in digital forensics that<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;"> **prevents any data from being written to a storage device** during investigation</mark>.

### **Key Points**

- Ensures the **original evidence is not modified**
    
- Allows **read-only access** to the device
    
- Maintains **data integrity** for legal purposes
    
- Used during the **acquisition phase** of Digital Forensics
    

### **In One Line**

> A write blocker lets investigators **<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">read data without changing anything on the original device</mark>**.
