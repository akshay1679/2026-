### Malware Analysis & Network Isolation (Using INetSim)

- **Malware Behavior**
    - Most malware relies on an active **network/internet connection**.
    - It may attempt to:
        - Communicate with command-and-control (C2) servers [[c2 server]]
        - Download additional payloads
        - Exfiltrate data
- **Security Practice: Network Isolation**
    - During malware analysis, it is important to:
        - **Disconnect the system from the real internet**
    - <mark style="background: #FF5582A6;">This prevents</mark>:
        - Accidental data leaks
        - Further infection or spread
        - Communication with malicious servers
- **Challenge**
    - <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Some malware</mark> may:
        - <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Detect lack of internet connectivity</mark>
        - Stop execution or hide behavior
- **Solution: Simulated Network Environment**
    - To observe full malware behavior:
        - We simulate an internet connection instead of providing a real one
- **Tool Used: INetSim**
    - INetSim (Internet Services Simulation Suite) is used to:
        - Mimic common internet services (HTTP, DNS, FTP, etc.)
        - <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Trick malware into believing it is connected to the internet</mark>
- **Benefits of Using INetSim**
    - Safe environment for analysis
    - Enables observation of malware network activity
    - Logs all simulated communications
    - Prevents real-world damage
