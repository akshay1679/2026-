## **3. Types of Evidence in Digital Forensics**

### **1. Deleted Files**

- Files that were removed but can often be recovered
    
- Stored in unallocated disk space
    
- Tools can reconstruct or recover them
    

---

### **2. Timestamps**

- Provide timing information for files and system events
    
- Common types:
    
    - Created time
        
    - Modified time
        
    - Accessed time
        
- Help in building timelines
    

---

### **3. User Activity**

- Tracks actions performed by users
    
- Includes:
    
    - Login/logout records
        
    - Browser history
        
    - Application usage
        
- Helps identify suspicious behavior
    

---

### **4. Unauthorized Traffic**

- Network activity that is abnormal or malicious
    
- Examples:
    
    - Unknown IP connections
        
    - Data exfiltration
        
    - Suspicious downloads/uploads
        

---

## **4. Acquisition in Digital Forensics**

### **What is Acquisition?**

Acquisition is the process of collecting and copying digital evidence from a device for analysis.

---

### **Key Concepts**

#### **1. Copying Evidence**

- A **forensic image** (exact bit-by-bit copy) is created
    
- Ensures all data is preserved, including hidden and deleted data
    

---

#### **2. Legal Hold**

- A process to preserve data for legal purposes
    
- Prevents deletion, modification, or tampering of evidence
    
- Ensures compliance with legal requirements
    

---

#### **3. No Changes to Original System**

- The original system is called **“evidence source”**
    
- Investigators must:
    
    - **Never modify the original system**
        
    - Avoid booting or altering it unnecessarily
        

---

#### **4. Working on Copies Only**

- All analysis is performed on the copied data
    
- Reasons:
    
    - Preserve integrity of evidence
        
    - Maintain admissibility in court
        
    - Avoid accidental data loss
        

---

### **Important Principle**

> **“Do not touch the original evidence — always work on a verified copy.”**
