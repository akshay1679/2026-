**MTTD (Mean Time To Detect)** is a key cybersecurity metric that measures **how long it takes to detect a security incident or threat after it occurs**.

---

## 🔍 Simple Definition

👉 **MTTD = Average time between when an attack starts and when it is detected**

---

## 🧠 Why MTTD Matters

- The **faster you detect**, the **less damage attackers can do**
    
- A **low MTTD** means:
    
    - Strong monitoring systems
        
    - Efficient threat detection
        
- A **high MTTD** means:
    
    - Threats stay hidden longer
        
    - More risk of data breaches
        

---

## 📊 Formula

[  
MTTD = \frac{\text{Total time to detect incidents}}{\text{Number of incidents}}  
]

---

## 📌 Example

- Incident 1 detected in **2 hours**
    
- Incident 2 detected in **4 hours**
    
- Incident 3 detected in **6 hours**
    

[  
MTTD = (2 + 4 + 6) / 3 = 4 \text{ hours}  
]

---

## 🔐 In Real Cybersecurity Systems

MTTD is influenced by tools like:

- SIEM (Security Information and Event Management)
    
- IDS/IPS (Intrusion Detection/Prevention Systems)
    
- EDR (Endpoint Detection & Response)
    

---

## ⚖️ Related Metrics (Important for Exams 🔥)

- **MTTR (Mean Time To Respond)** → Time to respond after detection
    
- **MTTC (Mean Time To Contain)** → Time to contain the threat
    
- **MTTR (Mean Time To Recover)** → Time to fully recover
    

---

## 🚀 Quick Summary

- MTTD measures **detection speed**
    
- Lower MTTD = **better security posture**
    
- Critical for SOC (Security Operations Center)
