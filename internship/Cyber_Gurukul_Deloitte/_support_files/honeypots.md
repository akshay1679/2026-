**Honeypots** are **decoy computer systems or servers set up to attract hackers**.

They look like real targets, but their purpose is to **detect, monitor, and study attacks** without risking the real network. 🍯💻

**In short:**

> A **honeypot is a fake system used to lure hackers so security teams can observe and stop them.**
