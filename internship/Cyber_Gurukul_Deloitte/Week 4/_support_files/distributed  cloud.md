**1. Ownership** → Usually provider (sometimes shared)  
**2. Users** → Organizations needing local processing  
**3. Location** → Many small sites near users (edge)  
**4. Why choose it** → Low latency, local data rules  
**5. Challenge** → Managing many locations consistently

👉 Think: **many small branches of the same store**.
