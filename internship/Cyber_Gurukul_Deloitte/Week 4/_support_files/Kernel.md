The kernel is the <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">core of the OS</mark>. It is the first major piece of software loaded at boot.

Its responsibilities include:

- **Process management** – starting, stopping, scheduling programs.
- **Memory management** – who gets RAM, protection between programs.
- **Device drivers** – talking to disks, USB, graphics, network.
- **Filesystems** – reading/writing files.
- **Networking** – sockets, packets.
- **Security & permissions**.
