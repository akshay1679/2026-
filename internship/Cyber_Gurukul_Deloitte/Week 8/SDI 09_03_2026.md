## Intelligence vs Information vs Data


Data --> In its raw form generally exists in huge volume, defines an object or individual, does not include any context, and is unprocessed.

- Firewall logs
- System logs
- Network packets
- Login records
- Security alerts
- System alerts

who owns that IP address
whether it is mal. or not?
why the connection happened?
---> data alone cannot help us to make the security decisions in org.



Information --> Interconnected bunch of raw data providing meaningful information, which states facts about human queries and knowledge.

- when did it happened?
- where did it happened?
- IP-45.72.78.62 attempted a connect to port 445 on 50 different servers
- multiple connection attempts occurred.
- the activity targeted a specific port 445
- this behaviour is suspicious
	- this processed data becomes information

Intelligence --> Analyzed and interpreted information providing broader in-depth knowledge of the subject, that supports decision making and response actions.


- why did that attack occurred? who is behind of that attack? what step we need to take next?
- that particular IP is associated with a known malware campaign. attackers are targeting the port no. 445 to exploit the vuln. SMB
- block the IP address monitor the similar types of activity patch the vuln. SMB
