
## Single Loss Expectancy (SLE)

<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">SLE = av * ef </mark>

**SLE = AV × EF**

- **AV (Asset Value):** What the asset is worth (in money).
    
- **EF (Exposure Factor):** The % of the asset lost in a single incident (expressed as a decimal).

---
### 📌 Formulas

- **SLE = AV × EF**
    
- **ARO = <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Annualized Rate of Occurrence</mark>**
    
- **ALE = SLE × ARO** (<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Annualized Loss Expectancy</mark>)
    

### 🎯 Why this matters

ALE helps you decide whether a control is worth it:

- If a security control costs **$5k/year** and reduces ALE from **$10k → $2k**, it’s a win.
    
- If it costs **$20k/year**, probably not worth it.

## 1️⃣ Asset Value (AV)

**What it is:**  
<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">How much the asset is worth.</mark>

**Example:**  
A company database is worth **$50,000**.  
➡️ **AV = $50,000**

---

## 2️⃣ Exposure Factor (EF)

**What it is:**  
<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">How much of the asset is lost in one incident</mark> (as a percentage).

**Example:**  
A breach would damage **20%** of the database.  
➡️ **EF = 0.20**

---

## 3️⃣ Single Loss Expectancy (SLE)

**What it is:**  
<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Loss from **one** incident.</mark>

**Formula:**  
**SLE = AV × EF**

**Example:**  
$50,000 × 0.20 = **$10,000**  
➡️ **SLE = $10,000**

---

## 4️⃣ Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO)

**What it is:**  
<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">How often the incident happens **per year**.</mark>

**Example:**  
The breach happens **once every 5 years**.  
➡️ **ARO = 0.2**

---

## 5️⃣ Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)

**What it is:**  
<mark style="background: #FFB86CA6;">Expected loss **per year**.</mark>

**Formula:**  
**ALE = SLE × ARO**

**Example:**  
$10,000 × 0.2 = **$2,000/year**  
➡️ **ALE = $2,000**

---

## 🧠 One-Line Summary (Exam Gold)

- **AV:** value of asset

- **EF:** % lost per incident

- **SLE:** loss per incident

- **ARO:** incidents per year

- **ALE:** loss per year
