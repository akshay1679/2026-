**Transposition cryptography** is a method of encryption where the **positions of characters are rearranged** according to a specific system — but the actual characters themselves are **not changed**.

So unlike substitution ciphers (where letters are replaced with other letters), transposition ciphers simply **shuffle the order** of the letters.
