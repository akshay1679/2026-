**Auth0** is a cloud platform that handles **authentication and authorization** for applications.

In plain terms, it lets you add things like:

- logging in with email/password
    
- social login (Google, GitHub, etc.)
    
- multi-factor authentication (codes, biometrics)
    
- single sign-on across multiple apps
    
- issuing and verifying tokens (JWT, OAuth)
    

without you having to build and secure all of that infrastructure yourself.
